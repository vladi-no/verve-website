Perspective / 02

Starting with the material.

We worked with Parador's designers and drew each room from the mood of its floor.

We worked with Parador’s designers to build each room around the character of its floor. A product is shaped by everything around it — the space it occupies, the materials beside it, and the way light moves through it.

Verve brings those elements together to give each story its own sense of place.

For each room, we started with the floor. Favo’s pattern became the geometry of the space. Ripplum’s grain suggested still water. The dark lines of Nexum brought to mind a forest. From there, we explored different room compositions until each product found a setting that felt natural to it.

The result is a visual world where product and space belong together — considered, distinctive, and at home in their surroundings.

Collaboration / 03

A real floor, not a simulation.

An editorial collaboration exploring how Parador’s floors translate into crafted spaces.

We filmed and photographed each floor in our studio. Working from Parador's floor mappings, we built surfaces that answer to light the way the boards do. Harmonia, the collection these rooms were made for, went on to receive the Red Dot Award: Product Design 2026.

The editorial study looks closely at shifting daylight, tactile surfaces and the space between an object and its setting. Still imagery and film work together to create a cohesive portrait of the collection.

Motion / 04

Objects in motion.

Verve explores how movement, rhythm and sound can bring a product story to life, extending its Atlanta studio's practice from still imagery into film.

Movement can reveal the character of a product in ways a still image cannot. A considered camera move, a change in pace or a small shift in light can draw attention to form and finish.

Verve brings those choices together with sound and editing to make product films that feel connected to the wider visual story.
